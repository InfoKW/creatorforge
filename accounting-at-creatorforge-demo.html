Foundation 1 of 5

Open Your LLC

▶
3 min · "Why I tell every creator to do this in week one"
— Luc, CreatorForge

Right now the money coming in is probably going straight to your personal account. That's the fastest way to overpay the IRS and the fastest way to get audited if a bank or the government ever looks closely at what's "you" versus what's "the business."

An LLC costs a few hundred dollars and takes about two days once we have your info. It's the line between your personal life and everything you're building.

Kelli's team handles the filing — you just tell us the business name and state, and it's done. No lawyer, no guessing.

Foundation 2 of 5

Open a Business Bank Account

▶
2 min · "The single biggest bookkeeping mistake creators make"
— Luc, CreatorForge

Once the LLC exists, open a bank account in the business name and move every dollar of Shop income through it. Nothing personal touches this account — not your Starbucks, not your rent.

Why it matters: at tax time, your bookkeeper reads this account like a book. Mixed accounts mean guessing, and guessing means either overpaying the IRS or missing write-offs you earned.

We can introduce you directly to a community bank we work with — small enough that a real person picks up the phone.

Foundation 3 of 5

Get a Business Credit Card

▶
2 min · "The card I personally use — and why the fee pays for itself"
— Luc, CreatorForge

Think of every legitimate business expense the same way you think about commission. If you're fighting for a 1-2% commission bump on a product, but you're missing 15% of your spend in write-offs because there's no paper trail — that write-off is worth more than the commission fight.

A dedicated business card creates that paper trail automatically, and the right card's perks (points, insurance, cash back) often cover its own annual fee.

Luc's pick: Amex Business Gold/Platinum. If you'd rather build credit first, we also work with a credit repair partner — no judgment, just a plan.
